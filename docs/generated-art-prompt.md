## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Chapter 1 scrapbook cover concept for a top-down 2D exploration game
Primary request: Create an original, modern detailed pixel-art summer neighborhood scene inspired only by the broad atmosphere of the supplied Pennsylvania aerial and street references, not an exact real location.
Scene/backdrop: A fictionalized green semi-rural suburb on a bright summer afternoon, curving residential road, broad uneven lawns, mature deciduous and evergreen trees, deep house setbacks, long driveways, mailboxes, shrubs, flower beds, one parked car, distant farm fields, and a creek-woods edge.

The structures, roofs, driveways, and landscaping must differentiate them beyond color.
Style/medium: Cohesive original top-down three-quarter modern pixel art, 32 px tile-grid logic, crisp 2 px detail rhythm, readable at small scrapbook-cover size, nostalgic handheld-adventure warmth without copying any existing game.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape overview with the curved road as the orientation spine and the woods/creek entrance visible behind Billy's west side. No people.
Lighting/mood: Bright northwest afternoon light, short cool southeast shadows, inviting and peaceful.
Color palette: Rich summer greens, blue-gray asphalt, cream/blue homes, warm paper-friendly highlights.
Constraints: Fictionalize all geometry and omit house numbers/private details. No exact tracing. No logos, text, UI, labels, watermark, or photorealism.
Avoid: identical rectangular houses, flat empty lawns, evenly spaced trees, oversized roads, large text labels, inconsistent one-off tile styles.
